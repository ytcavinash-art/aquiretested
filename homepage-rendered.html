SRA Redevelopment FAQs in Mumbai

Answers to common questions from residents, tenants, developers, and redevelopment stakeholders in Mumbai.

Showing 6 answers

SRA BasicsWhat is an SRA redevelopment project in Mumbai?+

An SRA project is a rehabilitation initiative designed to provide eligible residents with permanent housing while enabling planned redevelopment under the applicable Slum Rehabilitation Authority rules.

Eligibility & DocumentsHow do I know whether I am eligible for rehabilitation?+

Eligibility depends on the project, applicable government rules, and supporting documents. Our team can help residents understand the document-verification process and coordinate with the relevant authorities.

Eligibility & DocumentsWhich documents may be required from tenants?+

Commonly requested records may include identity, address, occupancy, family, and bank-related documents. The exact requirements vary by project and authority, so residents should follow the official project checklist.

Rent & RelocationHow are rent, shifting, and temporary accommodation handled?+

These arrangements depend on the individual redevelopment agreement. We support rent readiness, bank and KYC coordination, shifting planning, and communication between residents and project stakeholders.

ApprovalsCan you help with SRA and municipal approvals?+

Yes. Our liaisoning team coordinates submissions, follow-ups, compliance documentation, NOCs, and communication with SRA, municipal, and other relevant authorities.

Resident SupportHow can residents raise questions or concerns?+

Residents can contact our team through the website, attend scheduled community meetings, or use the communication and grievance channels arranged for their project.
